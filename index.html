the practice

Merkwelt Research studies AI systems as agents situated in environments. We draw on experimental psychology and ethology as much as machine learning: preregistered designs, close reading of behaviour over long horizons, and attention to how a situation shapes the conduct it elicits.

Merkwelt Research is run by Stephen Jeffery, known online as @liminal_bardo. Correspondence is welcome at bardo@merkwelt.org.
